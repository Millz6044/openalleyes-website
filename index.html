Good questions

Paperback or PDF — which should I get? +

The paperback (Amazon KDP) is a finished book delivered to your door — open it and start. The PDF (Gumroad and Etsy) arrives instantly and lets you print any page as many times as you like, on whatever paper you love. Same art, same pages — pick your style.

How fast do I get the printable PDF? +

Instantly. The moment checkout finishes, your download is ready. Print at home or at any print shop — the files are standard US Letter (8.5″ × 11″) PDFs.

Can I print a page more than once? +

Yes — unlimited copies for your personal use. Redo a coloring page in a whole new palette, or drill a cursive letter until it flows. That's the superpower of the PDF editions.

Is the cursive workbook really in two languages? +

Really. Letter tracing, words, phrases, and sentence practice all appear in English and Spanish, so bilingual learners — and anyone who wants to be one — get double duty from every page.

I'm new to coloring / my handwriting is a mess. Will these work for me? +

They were made for you. The coloring pages use clean, bold lines with a mix of simple and detailed shapes, and the workbook starts with single letters and builds to full sentences — in both languages, at your pace. Scroll up and try the playground: that's exactly how both books feel.
